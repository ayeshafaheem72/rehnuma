LEAVE POLICY. All permanent employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at the rate of one and two-thirds days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward into the next year; any balance above that is forfeited on 31 December.

Sick leave. Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Maternity and paternity leave. Female employees are entitled to 90 days of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the birth.

Requesting leave. Annual leave should be requested through the HR portal at least 14 days in advance and needs written approval from the line manager. In an emergency the employee must inform their manager by phone before the start of their shift.

Unpaid leave. Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted at the discretion of the department head for compelling personal reasons. Unpaid leave does not count towards service for the purpose of gratuity.
