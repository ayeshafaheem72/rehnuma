SITE SAFETY POLICY - WAREHOUSE OPERATIONS

1. Personal protective equipment. Every person entering the loading area must wear a hi-visibility vest, safety boots with steel toe caps and a hard hat. Visitors must be escorted by a member of staff at all times and must wear the same equipment.

2. Forklift operation. Only staff holding a valid forklift licence may operate a forklift. Pedestrians must keep at least three metres away from a moving forklift and must never walk beneath a raised load. The speed limit inside the building is 8 km/h.

3. Manual handling. Loads over 20 kg must be lifted by two people or with a hoist. Keep the back straight, bend the knees and hold the load close to the body. Report any strain or injury to the shift supervisor before the end of the shift.

4. Fire safety. In the event of an alarm, stop work, switch off equipment and walk to the assembly point at the north car park. Do not use the lifts. The fire warden will take a headcount and report to the emergency services. Fire drills are held every quarter.

5. Incident reporting. All accidents and near misses must be reported within 24 hours using the online incident form. A near miss is an event that could have caused harm but did not. Reporting near misses helps prevent the next accident.
